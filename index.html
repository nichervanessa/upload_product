Open this from the shop computer

You are looking at the published copy of this page. It cannot reach your shop's system from here — a secure page is not allowed to talk to a plain address on your own network, and the browser blocks it silently.

On a phone connected to the shop's Wi-Fi, open:

http://SHOP-PC-IP:8000/add

To find the address, on the shop computer press Windows + R, type cmd, then type ipconfig and read the IPv4 Address — it looks like 192.168.1.50. The shop's system must be running, and the phone must be on the same Wi-Fi.
